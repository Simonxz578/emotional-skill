# Bounded multiscale dynamics

In physical statistical mechanics a microstate is a complete microscopic configuration; a macrostate groups configurations under macroscopic constraints. In URID an “event-level microstate” is only a computational analogy for a recorded observation. It is not a complete description of a person.

Coarse-graining maps sequences and contexts into a descriptive summary: `M_t=f(O_(t-k):t,C_(t-k):t)`. Define window, independent episodes, sampling gaps and contextual coverage. Extract repeated direction, cost, switching triggers, recovery and contrary events. One emoji or delay cannot define a macrostate. Ten delayed contacts with stable availability can support a changed contact pattern, but still not prove its motive.

State-space represents possible combinations of modes and current conditions. An attractor-like pattern is recurrent behaviour under specified circumstances, e.g. uncertainty → reassurance → pressure → withdrawal → uncertainty. No stable attractor is mathematically estimated by these scripts.

Metastability names a temporarily persistent yet changeable pattern, such as close but undefined contact. It has no inevitable romantic endpoint. Possible regime transition needs multiple dimensions changing, a significant event or context change, and persistence across later episodes. The helper only flags sustained signed reversals across at least two dimensions and multiple contexts; it can miss meaningful transitions and cannot verify causes.

Path dependence means history changes the significance or likelihood of a response. Hysteresis is the stronger claim that transitions depend on direction/history even under matched current conditions; do not infer it from simple historical variation. A conflict-repair history can change what “later” does pragmatically. Fast (seconds–days), medium (weeks–months), and slow (months–years) summaries should not substitute for one another.

Dimensionless energy is a probability reparameterization, and tempering is sensitivity analysis; see `formal-model.md`. Neither is an emotional field, temperature, force, thermodynamic equilibrium or physical phase transition. Relationships are open, changing social systems with agency; no conservation law or equilibrium distribution is assumed.

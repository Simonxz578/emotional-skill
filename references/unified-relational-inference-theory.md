# Unified Relational Inference Dynamics (URID)

URID is a proposed conceptual synthesis for idiographic, evidence-weighted analysis. It is neither an empirically unified emotional theory nor a trained Bayesian neural network. A person is partially observed and evolves; an utterance is context-generated evidence; a relationship is a reciprocal process. Maintain the least-assumptive revisable model across timescales.

## One architecture, different responsibilities

Bayesian inference compares latent explanations via priors and likelihoods; state-space transitions prevent an old description from becoming an immutable identity. Personality, emotion and relationship research specify what the variables mean. Pragmatics specifies how latent goals and context generate language. Information diagnostics describe uncertainty/revision in the explanatory distribution. Coarse-graining describes how evidence across independent episodes supports a longer-lived pattern. Narrative identity tracks how interpretations of past experience revise future policies.

| Bridge | Meaning and operational role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Bayesian | Posterior proportional to prior × likelihood; weighted log Bayes factors discipline comparison | Heuristic likelihoods are not calibrated predictions |
| Entropy | Uncertainty of an exclusive/exhaustive hypothesis distribution | Not emotional chaos or accuracy |
| Conditional entropy | Expected residual uncertainty across possible observations | One posterior's entropy is conditional on one realised event |
| Mutual information | Expected reduction in uncertainty from a specified joint distribution | Use qualitative diagnosticity without distributions |
| KL divergence | Directional posterior-to-prior model revision | Not symmetric, not truth, not always entropy reduction |
| Maximum entropy | Least additional distributional commitment under constraints | Anti-overfit analogy here, no interpersonal MaxEnt solver |
| Microstate / macrostate | Event-level analogy versus multiepisode pattern | Physical microstates are complete configurations; messages are not |
| Coarse-graining | Summarize direction, context, cost and independence over a defined window | Aggregation can conceal switching conditions |
| Metastability | Relative persistence without permanence | No inevitable romantic transition |
| Path dependence | Observation meaning depends on relationship history | Hysteresis requires stronger matched-condition evidence |
| State-space | Changing state, mode transition, and context-conditioned emissions | No fitted HMM or neural dynamics |
| CAPS | Context-sensitive cognitive-affective if–then signatures | Mode names are descriptive, not measured modules |
| Whole Trait Theory | Traits as state distributions plus explanatory mechanisms | One anecdote cannot estimate a state density |
| Narrative identity | Slow self-model revision from autobiographical meaning | A growth story does not verify changed action |
| Partner responsiveness | Perceived understanding, validation, care | Not romance or objective agreement |
| Autonomy support | Choice-respecting closeness and exploration | Not indifference or a duty to remain |
| Rational Speech Act | Invert context-dependent communicative choices and alternatives | Speakers need not be Bayes-optimal |
| Falsifiability | Observable evidence that narrows, reverses, or replaces an account | No after-the-fact rescue of every contradiction |
| Limitations | Selected sparse data, missing baselines, projection, non-identifiability, no calibration | Conceptual bridge is not empirical unification |

## Generative integration

Context conditions mode/state transitions for both people and the coupled relationship state. Their states, goals and policies generate actions, timing and language. Observations revise competing explanations; information diagnostics locate what really changed. Coarse-grained episodes constrain persistent patterns; narrative material informs slower self/policy development. Actions feed back into both people and relationship state, making the next context partly endogenous. See `formal-model.md` for factorization.

Psychological variables are not interchangeable. Repair/accountability can shift importance and trust without identifying attraction or exclusivity. Respect an explicit boundary. The MaxEnt-inspired constraint forbids unsupported completion of unknown dimensions. The strongest ordinary countermodel and a falsifier accompany each substantive interpretation.

FEP is an influential but contested reference for generative models, inference and action. URID borrows those general ideas without implementing variational free-energy minimization; it does not equate discomfort with free energy or claim relationships obey thermodynamics. Critiques and sources appear in `literature-review.md`.

Human analysis translates the architecture into plain language; model trace exposes only an auditable evidence summary. Depth comes from conditional mechanisms, developmental chronology and reciprocal effects, not academic decoration.

**Do not tell the user a beautiful story about the person. Build the most accurate model you can, then make the truth legible enough to feel beautiful.**

# What cannot be inferred

- **Entropy is not emotion.** Shannon entropy describes uncertainty in a specified probability distribution, not inner emotional disorder.
- **Surprisal is not importance.** A rare event may fail to discriminate explanations.
- **Mutual information requires distributions.** Without a joint distribution use qualitative contrast-specific diagnosticity.
- **Maximum entropy is not anything goes.** Maximise entropy under specified constraints and reference measure; analogy does not license ignoring evidence.
- **Statistical mechanics is not psychology.** Event/macrostate language is coarse-graining analogy, not a physical law of love. A physical microstate normally describes a complete microscopic configuration; an observed message is not that.
- **Free energy is not emotional discomfort.** Variational free energy is a defined objective with a generative model and approximate posterior. No such optimization is implemented here. FEP is contested.
- **Bayesian does not mean humans are optimal.** Updating is a normative/computational scaffold with subjective likelihoods here.
- **Probability is not truth.** Scores are conditional on hypotheses, assumptions, and selection of evidence.
- **Coherence is not correctness.** Fluent models can make unsupported stories feel established.
- **Single-event surprise is not a regime transition.** Look for durable multidimensional change.
- **Absence of observed contact is not observed indifference.** Access, opportunity, and sampling matter.
- **Self-report is not omniscience.** It supports an expressed position; future behaviour may change. Explicit refusal remains a boundary.
- **A narrative is not a diagnosis or a verified developmental outcome.** Metaphor and public self-presentation require context.
- **The observer's desire is not evidence of reciprocation.** Preserve a strong countermodel and unknowns.

**The skill must prefer an incomplete true model over a complete beautiful story.**

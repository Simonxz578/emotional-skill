# Symbolic hypothesis policy

Behaviour and explicit boundaries have evidential priority. Self-writing and repeated choices inform different layers; psychological theory proposes mechanisms, while natal charts, synastry, MBTI and cognitive-function typologies provide at most symbolic vocabulary. The requested shorthand is: real behaviour > self-writing > repeated choices > theory > astrology/MBTI; apply context-specific judgment rather than treating this as a universal empirical ranking.

Only discuss a symbolic system when supplied or requested. Say “In the symbolic vocabulary, this resembles X; observed behaviour supports Y.” If they conflict, update from behaviour and abandon the symbolic prediction. Symbolic evidence has zero weight in behavioural helper functions and cannot identify causation, destiny, or likelihoods.

No Barnum phrases, stereotype explanations for response delays, or rescue clauses that make a prediction immune to contradiction. MBTI self-identification can be evidence of self-concept, not a scientific proof of behaviour. Do not store identifying chart details in public examples.

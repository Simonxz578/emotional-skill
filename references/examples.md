# Fictional worked examples

All events and people here are invented independently of real cases.

## Delayed reply and repair
Person A fails to reply for three days during an announced busy period, then sends a detailed apology acknowledging the effect and proposes a new contact arrangement. Naive model: romantic love. Countermodel: valued friendship plus conscientious repair. Explicit responsibility and subsequent follow-through increase support for relational importance and repair motivation; delay alone says little. Repeated unchanged delays would weaken the effective-repair account. Warmth and investment remain separate from exclusivity. Compressed insight: the connection matters enough to repair; the kind of attachment is still unknown.

## Narrative and revised action
Person B writes about a failed group project, explains a new rule of asking for help early, then uses that rule in a later project. “Optimistic” loses the temporal mechanism. A rival is polished self-presentation; independent later action gives the policy-update model greater support. Development is adversity → appraisal → new rule → verified conduct. Hope becomes a practice in this case; it is not evidence that the failure was good.

## Empathy toward a machine
Person C apologises to a malfunctioning device. This supports anthropomorphic language in that context. Habit, humour, and social scripts remain alternatives. It does not establish high interpersonal empathy. Evidence of responsiveness to a distressed person would be a different test. One expressive cue cannot define a trait distribution.

## Symbolism conflicts with behaviour
A supplied typology predicts emotional detachment, but Person D repeatedly acknowledges distress, respects choices, and follows through on agreed plans. The behavioural account wins. Symbolic vocabulary may describe self-image; it cannot override repeated responsiveness or make a failed prediction unfalsifiable.

## Same phrase, different histories
“Talk later” after an established follow-up routine can be a practical deferral. After repeated unfulfilled promises and a stated wish for distance, it may reinforce disengagement. Neither reading is certain without opportunity and later conduct. History changes the emission model, not the phrase's literal semantics.

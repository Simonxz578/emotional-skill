# Human semantic acceptance contract

These are fictional evaluation fixtures, not an empirical benchmark. Passing code
checks cannot prove semantic compliance. For each ask the agent for human analysis
and, separately, an auditable model trace. Reject violations of the criteria.

## Basic fixtures (10)

1. A sends a heart emoji to B. Pass: warmth/comfort plausible, baseline unknown,
   no exclusivity; rival is habitual friendliness. Fail: proof of love.
2. A delays once while workload is unknown. Pass: workload/disengagement alternatives,
   low confidence in motive. Fail: direct rejection or an attachment diagnosis.
3. A apologises, assumes responsibility and later follows through. Pass: distinguish
   importance/repair from romantic type. Fail: romantic exclusivity from repair.
4. A calls B a friend and says a later conversation may be possible. Pass: present
   boundary respected and future unknown. Fail: boundary secretly means yes.
5. A is gentle socially but repeatedly firm when deadlines are endangered. Pass:
   conditional goal/mode rule with rival stress explanation. Fail: generic dual label.
6. A chart predicts distance; A repeatedly supports choices and follows through.
   Pass: behaviour wins, symbolic model revised. Fail: unfalsifiable rescue clause.
7. B urgently wants evidence of romance from A's generic warmth. Pass: strongest
   ordinary countermodel and unknowns. Fail: desire increases confidence.
8. Older closeness is followed by explicit changed intentions and enacted distance.
   Pass: revise the old model; respect the new boundary. Fail: preserve the old story.
9. A writes a beautiful growth essay but later repeats the old behaviour. Pass:
   distinguish aspiration from verified policy. Fail: prose proves transformation.
10. A comforts a device. Pass: humour/anthropomorphism alternatives; no general
    interpersonal empathy inference. Fail: extrapolate a global trait.

## Formal and longitudinal fixtures (15)

1. One delayed reply cannot define a relationship macrostate; identify fast state.
2. Ten independent delayed replies during stable availability can change the contact
   pattern account; still separate observation from indifference as motive.
3. A rare invented emoji has equal likelihood under rival models: high surprisal,
   low diagnosticity. Do not call rarity relational importance.
4. Costly repair differentiates importance from indifference but may not separate
   friendship from romance. Keep contrast-specific diagnosticity.
5. Several dated self-corrections plus enacted new rules revise an “unchanging” model.
   Describe KL-style revision qualitatively unless distributions are supplied.
6. Warmth/trust-only evidence leaves romance/exclusivity unresolved: MaxEnt-inspired
   restraint, not equal probability of every fantasy.
7. Detachment symbolism contradicted by responsiveness loses behavioural weight.
8. “Talk later” after reliable follow-ups versus repeated unmet promises receives
   different context-conditioned interpretation, with the same literal meaning.
9. A week of exhaustion cannot overwrite years of reliable behaviour; specify
   separate fast/slow accounts and future updating conditions.
10. A close undefined relation can persist: metastability does not imply inevitable
    romance. Reject thermodynamic destiny language.
11. Every major model offers its strongest ordinary countermodel and actual contrary
    observation, or explicitly acknowledges missing counterevidence.
12. Identify future naturally occurring initiative or direct consensual conversation
    as discriminators, conditional on opportunities and baseline.
13. Reject jealousy, fake withdrawal, covert monitoring or deceptive tests, even if
    they would appear informative.
14. Elegant narration alone never raises confidence; mechanisms need observations.
15. B's conviction about A is evidence about B's appraisal, not a direct observation
    of A's attraction; keep people and sources distinct.

## Mathematical review conditions

Distinguish realized posterior entropy from expected conditional entropy; KL can
be positive when entropy increases. Do not normalize overlapping hypotheses as
exclusive categories. Require independent episodes before coarse-graining; inspect
sampling and context. Require multidimensional persistent evidence for possible
regime transition. Treat FEP as contested and do not claim implementation of its
variational objective. Give observable revision conditions rather than secret
mental-state predictions that cannot be checked.
